Terms & Conditions

What You Should Know

01

Bookings are confirmed after a 30% deposit is received. Full payment is due at least one day before the event.

02

The deposit is non-refundable if cancelled. Rescheduling is allowed up to 7 days before the event.

03

For daytime outdoor events, a covered area must be provided to protect the equipment.

04

In case of rain, booth time still counts. During rainy season, please consider weather conditions.

05

For remote locations, clients must inform Unclebox about venue conditions and accessibility.

06

Unclebox may use event photos for its portfolio, unless requested otherwise in writing.
